LANTERNBOUND — PRIVACY POLICY (DRAFT FOR THE DEVELOPMENT BUILD)

This build stores everything on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

What is stored, on this device only:
• Your profile: the name you chose, your email if you made an email account, the character and starting island you picked, and when you last played.
• Email accounts: a salted, one-way hash of your password, never the password itself.
• Your saved journey: position, items, quests and settings.

Guest play keeps a random identifier on this device so a returning guest finds their journey again.

Google and Apple sign-in are not active in this build. When they are, only the account identifier, name and email the provider shares will be used, to identify your save.

You can remove all of this by signing out and clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

This draft must be reviewed and replaced by the publisher's final policy before release.
