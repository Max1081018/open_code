# Lanternbound — design direction

## The pitch

The Lumen Cores under the hills are failing and the dark is rising. Where it
reaches, the old dead get up. You are the hooded wanderer who woke in the
meadow with a lantern still burning: the only light that moves. Everything
in the game is about carrying light into places that have lost it, and what
you find there.

## The loop

1. **Venture out** with a lantern that burns down (glow is a resource).
2. **Discover** something in every outing: a cave, a ruin, a sealed vault, a
   rare ore, a creature, a relic, a person.
3. **Bring it home**: craft, upgrade, plant wardstones that push the dark
   back and make safe ground.
4. **Go deeper**: better light and gear unlock harder regions, and each
   region hides a boss guarding a Lumen Core.

## Signature mechanics (ours, not borrowed)

- **Light is a weapon and a key.** The undead burn in strong light. The
  lantern's **Flare** spends glow in a burst that staggers the dead and
  makes hidden things answer: **light-runes** in walls that only show in
  lantern light mark secret rooms.
- **Sealed vaults**: rooms in ruins closed by a rune door that opens when
  every brazier inside is lit (torch, flare or fire). Puzzles of reach and
  order, not of reading.
- **Relics**: unique finds from vaults, bosses and secrets (never crafted),
  worn in three relic slots, each changing how you play (a second jump, ore
  sense, glow that regrows in the dark, an ember that sets struck foes
  burning...).
- **The Dark Tide**: some nights the dark surges. The dead rise out of the
  ground everywhere a light is not burning. Wardstones and lit bases are
  sanctuaries; a well-lit home is a real choice.

|---|---|---|---|

## Art direction

Real 3D characters (KayKit, CC0) filmed side-on into a 2D world of painted
tiles, layered parallax and light. Warm lantern light against cool dark;
saturated, readable silhouettes; everything that moves has a real animation.

## Rules for new work

- Every system feeds another (relics change how light, combat and mining
  play; vaults reward relics; the tide makes wardstones matter).
- Nothing is added only to make a list longer.
- Every feature gets a test, a capture, and a line in PROGRESS.md.
