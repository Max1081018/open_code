# Lanternbound — Realms of the Starfall: art bible

The game is an anime fantasy action RPG. Its reference points are modern anime open-world games
(bright skies, cel-ish characters, magical architecture), with an original identity: the
**Starfall** — fallen star-light that crystallised across the land.

## Mood
Wonder first. Clear blue skies and golden sunsets, white-and-gold crystal spires, floating isles
with waterfalls, glowing flowers, ancient star ruins. Danger is violet and black (the Void), never
brown and muddy. Nothing should read as Viking, rustic or "survival camp".

## Palette
| Role | Colours |
|---|---|
| Sky | saturated cerulean (#2961DB) to pale horizon (#B8D6FA); sunsets gold-pink-violet |
| Starlight (magic, UI accents) | gold #F5D68F, pale starlight #F2EEFF, cyan glow #6FD9FF |
| Void (enemies, corruption) | violet #7A4CFF, black-purple #1A1030, crimson accents |
| Foliage | fresh greens, never olive; flowers in lilac, white, sky blue |
| UI panels | deep indigo glass #171A38 at 90 %, gold 2 px edges, rounded 14–18 px |

## Characters
Anime proportions and faces, readable silhouettes (twin tails, capes, ribbons, horns), layered
fantasy clothing. Heights: heroine 1.56 m, youths 1.6 m, adults 1.7–1.8 m, bosses 2 m+.
Each character has its own idle, walk and speed (`CharacterKit.HERO_STYLE`).
Main heroine: silver-lavender twin tails, small horns, bat wings and tail, oversized black
jacket with lilac ribbons, plaid skirt (model a3, Sketchfab "3D Anime Character girl for
Blender" by CGBlender, CC BY). A male lead will follow.

## Equipment (replacing the rustic set)
Swords are elegant, with crystal and gold fittings; catalysts and staves glow. Gathering tools are
magical: a **starcutter blade** (trees, plants), a **crystal resonator** (ore and crystal nodes).
Resources are crystals, star-ore, luminous plants and void cores rather than logs and coal.
Crafting stations are altars and arcane workbenches with floating runes.

## World
Wide green valleys under huge skies; white-stone towns with blue and gold roofs; crystal
outcrops instead of plain boulders; star ruins (white stone, gold inlay, blue light pillars);
floating islands on the horizon. No digging underground: caves and dungeons are places you
enter.

## UI
Cinzel / Cinzel Decorative for titles and menus, Cormorant Italic for flavour lines. Gold
diamond markers, indigo glass panels, round icon buttons with thin gold rims. Icons are
renders of the real 3D items on transparent backgrounds.
